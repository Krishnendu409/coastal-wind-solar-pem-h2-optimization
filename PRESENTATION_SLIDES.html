⚡ Pillar 1: High-Current PEMWE Physics

  • Calibrated against experimental data from Bernt & Gasteiger (2016): $\text{RMSE} = 3.10\text{ mV}$, $R^2 = 0.99971$.
  • Exact Nernst reversible potential ($V_{\text{rev}} = 1.234\text{ V}$ at $75^\circ\text{C}, 30\text{ bar}$) using Antoine vapor pressure ($0.3846\text{ bar}$).
  • Anodic catalytic recombination ($\eta_{\text{recomb}} = 0.88$) limits $\text{H}_2$-in-$\text{O}_2 \le 1.64\text{ mol\%} < 2.0\text{ mol\%}$ ($50\%$ LEL limit).

🌿 Pillar 2: Green AI & Split-CAPEX Sizing

  • Strictly leak-free 12D physics surrogate trained on 6-year ($52,608\text{ h}$) NASA POWER reanalysis with zero cost leakage.
  • $89.94\%$ FLOP reduction over deep neural networks; Spearman $\rho = 0.9994$ Pareto rank preservation.
  • Split-CAPEX modeling proves high-current overload ($>2\text{ A/cm}^2$) increases LCOH due to peak BOP scaling.

🛡️ Pillar 3: Load-Compensated MSPM

  • 9-sensor industrial PCA benchmarked against load-compensated 1D voltage-residual EWMA across 100 Monte Carlo runs.
  • EWMA detects slightly faster ($10.6\text{ h}$ vs.\ $11.6\text{ h}$), but **PCA uniquely isolates root causes** via contribution plots.
  • Isolates thermal-pressure faults ($95.2\%$ contribution) during dehydration and overpotentials during flooding.

Slide 2 • Regional Climatology & Siting Regimes

Multi-Year Climatology: Coastal Solar vs. Wheeled Highland Wind

🌊 6-Year Meteorological Reality (2019–2024, 52,608 Hours)

  • On-Site Coastal Corridor (Udupi–Mangalore, $13.35^\circ\text{N}, 74.79^\circ\text{E}$):
    • Strong solar PV resource: Tilted POA $220.59\text{ W/m}^2$, $\overline{\text{CF}}_{\text{solar}} = 21.23\%$ ($24.82\%$ in summer).
    • Uninflated $100\text{ m}$ coastal wind speed averages only $4.15\text{ m/s}$, delivering $\overline{\text{CF}}_{\text{wind,coastal}} = \mathbf{14.70\%}$.
  • Inland Highland Wind Corridor (Chitradurga–Gadag, $14.23^\circ\text{N}, 76.40^\circ\text{E}$):
    • Highland wind speed averages $6.69\text{ m/s}$ ($\overline{\text{CF}}_{\text{wind,gen}} = 44.82\%$, surging to $60.99\%$ during monsoon).
    • Wheeled via KPTCL $220\text{ kV}$ grid ($3.4\%$ transmission loss, KERC tariff $₹0.58/\text{kWh}$) $\rightarrow$ delivered $\overline{\text{CF}} = \mathbf{43.29\%}$.

⚡ Siting Regime Comparison

  • Regime B (Wheeled Highland Wind + Coastal Solar): Achieves $\text{LCOH} = \mathbf{₹332.51/\text{kg H}_2}$ ($$3.96/\text{kg}$) with $\text{LPSP} = 0.62\%$.
  • Regime A (Pure Coastal Co-located): Requires larger battery and solar over-sizing, yielding $\text{LCOH} = \mathbf{₹382.04/\text{kg}}$ ($12.96\%$ more expensive).
  • Coastal Wind Drought Risk: Deploying the knee-point plant solely on coastal wind without wheeling causes catastrophic supply failure ($\text{LPSP} = \mathbf{21.30\%}$).

Slide 3 • First-Principles Electrochemistry & Experimental Calibration

Calibrated High-Current PEMWE Electrochemistry ($75^\circ\text{C}, 30\text{ bar}$)

$$V_{\text{cell}} = V_{\text{rev}}(T,P) + \eta_{\text{act}}(i,T) + \eta_{\text{ohm}}(i,T,\lambda) + \eta_{\text{conc}}(i,T)$$

⚙️ Physical Overpotential & Safety Formulation

  • Thermodynamic $V_{\text{rev}}$: Exact Antoine vapor pressure ($P_{\text{sat}} = 0.3846\text{ bar}$) $\rightarrow$ $V_{\text{rev}} = \mathbf{1.234\text{ V}}$ (correcting preliminary $1.217\text{ V}$ bug).
  • Butler–Volmer Activation ($\eta_{\text{act}}$): Nanostructured $\text{IrO}_2$ anode ($E_a = 42\text{ kJ/mol}$) and $\text{Pt/C}$ cathode ($E_a = 16\text{ kJ/mol}$) arcsinh kinetics.
  • Nafion 115 Ohmic ($\eta_{\text{ohm}}$): $\delta_{\text{mem}} = 127\ \mu\text{m}$, contact resistance $R_{\text{elec}} = 0.032\ \Omega\cdot\text{cm}^2$.
  • Bubble Mass-Transport ($\eta_{\text{conc}}$): $i_{L,\text{an}} = 4.2\text{ A/cm}^2$, bubble crowding coefficient $\beta_{\text{bub}} = 0.014\text{ V}\cdot\text{cm}^{3.3}/\text{A}^{1.65}$.
  • Turndown Safety: Catalytic recombination ($\eta_{\text{recomb}} = 0.88$) and $i_{\min} = 0.20\text{ A/cm}^2$ limit anodic $\text{H}_2$-in-$\text{O}_2 \le 1.64\text{ mol\%} < 2.0\text{ mol\%}$ ($50\%$ LEL).

Slide 4 • Green Deep Learning & Controlled Ablation

Strictly Leak-Free Green-MLP vs. Deep Neural Networks

🌱 Leak-Free Physics Feature Map

  • Eliminating Target Leakage: Formulated $\mathbf{\Phi}(\mathbf{x})$ containing zero cost coefficients—only dimensionless capacity ratios ($P_w/P_{\text{PEM}}, P_s/P_{\text{PEM}}, E_b/P_{\text{PEM}}$), nominal-to-peak current ratios ($i_{\max}/i_{\text{nom}}$), cell overvoltage, and Faradaic efficiency.
  • Computational Urgency: Evaluating 52,608 hours per design makes evolutionary search take hours without a surrogate. `Green-MLP` evaluates in $8.55\ \mu\text{s}$ ($\mathbf{4,206\times}$ speedup over Numba, $\mathbf{47,659\times}$ over Python ODE).

Slide 8 • Dynamic Multi-Year Dispatch Verification

Seasonal Operational Profiles: Summer Solar vs. Monsoon Wind

☀️ Pre-Monsoon Summer Week (April, 168-Hour Window)

  • Diurnal Solar Pulse: On-site solar PV peaks at $\sim 70\text{ MW}$ around noon.
  • Buffer Management: Daytime hydrogen generation ramps to $1,050\text{ kg/h}$, charging the $30\text{ bar}$ buffer tank to its $14,000\text{ kg}$ capacity to sustain continuous $500\text{ kg/h}$ delivery overnight.

🌧️ Southwest Monsoon Week (July, 168-Hour Window)

  • Highland Wind Baseload: Heavy monsoon cloud cover attenuates solar output, but sustained highland wind provides continuous $30\text{–}45\text{ MW}$ power delivery.
  • High-Efficiency Nominal Electrolysis: PEM stack operates steadily near nominal efficiency ($1.1\text{–}1.4\text{ A/cm}^2$, $\eta_{\text{LHV}} > 64\%$) delivering $480\text{–}540\text{ kg H}_2/\text{h}$ with minimal battery cycling.

Slide 9 • Conclusions & Policy Implications

Summary of Scientific Findings & Coastal Deployment Roadmap

🔬 Key Scientific Discoveries

  • 1. Regional Siting Advantage: Wheeled highland wind from Chitradurga–Gadag lowers LCOH by $\mathbf{12.96\%}$ relative to pure coastal generation ($\text{INR } 332.51/\text{kg}$ vs $\text{INR } 382.04/\text{kg}$) and avoids coastal wind drought failure ($\text{LPSP} = 21.30\%$).
  • 2. Split-CAPEX Overload Trade-off: Sizing rectifiers and BOP for peak overload power outweighs membrane area savings. Optimal knee point operates at $i_{\max}^* = 1.764\text{ A/cm}^2$; nominal current sizing yields $\text{INR } 329.12/\text{kg}$.
  • 3. Leak-Free Green AI Surrogate: `Green-MLP` ($4,612$ params) achieves $R^2 = 0.9943 \pm 0.0031$ and $\rho = 0.9994$ rank preservation, cutting forward FLOPs by $\mathbf{89.94\%}$ and accelerating simulation by $\mathbf{4,206\times}$.
  • 4. Diagnostic Realities: PCA does not detect earlier than load-compensated EWMA ($11.6\text{ h}$ vs $10.6\text{ h}$), but provides critical multi-sensor root-cause diagnosis ($95.2\%$ thermal-pressure isolation).

🏭 Industrial Deployment Roadmap for Karnataka

  • Phase 1 (Open-Access PPA): Execute green open-access power purchase agreement for $48\text{ MW}$ highland wind under KERC wheeling regulations.
  • Phase 2 (Co-Located Solar & Electrolysis): Deploy $73\text{ MW}$ tilted solar PV adjacent to industrial off-takers in Mangalore with $44\text{ MW}$ nominal PEMWE stack ($14\text{ t}$ buffer).
  • Phase 3 (Online Digital Twin & MSPM): Deploy `Green-MLP` for real-time dispatch scheduling and PCA $T^2/Q$ charts for early fault diagnosis.
